## [3.0.0] - 2026-11-20

### Added

- Crucible: a clay pot that melts metal over heat. Drop metal into it, set it over a campfire/fire pit or stove for soft
  metals, lava or magma for iron and steel, then pick it up and pour the molten metal into a mould.
- Moulds for ingots, plates, vessels, frames, and pipes: place one down, pour metal in from a crucible, a tap, a pipe or
  a Spout, and take the casting out once it cools.
- Smeltery: a big lava-fuelled furnace. It melts eight things at once, gets more out of raw ore, mixes molten metals
  into alloys, and makes rare metals. Hang a tap on its side to pour into moulds.
- Ponders for the crucible and smeltery.
- All horses have the same speed and jump strength, matching the best a horse could have before.
- Animals and mobs can sit on stools. Any seated mob can run a Stock Ticker.
- Sitting down makes stamina recover twice as fast.
- Grinding in a mortar and using a workstone takes stamina.
- Zombie leaders drop 2-3 Crystal Shards when killed by a player, more with Looting.
- New hidden advancements.
- A Create deployer holding a hammer can use a workstone.
- The clock overlay shows how cloudy the sky is.
- Hold right-click on a Basin with a Spoon to mix its contents by hand: anything a Mechanical Mixer can make, except
  superheated recipes.
- Potions are brewed in a heated Basin with a Spoon, or automatically with a Mechanical Mixer. A full Basin fills four
  bottles, and arrows stirred into a brew become Tipped Arrows.
- Using dyed leather, banners, dyed shulker boxes, or labelled items on a Basin of water washes them clean, like a water
  cauldron used to.
- Pointed Dripstone under lava fills a Basin below with a full bucket per drop.
- Water dripping from Pointed Dripstone into a heated Basin boils away and leaves Salt behind.
- New ponders for brewing in a Basin, stirring a Basin, and dripstone lava and salt farms.
- Parkour with ParCool: wall jumps, wall runs, horizontal wall runs, vaults, long jumps, charge jumps, dives, skydives,
  crawling, slides, hanging, climbing up, castaways, sliding down walls, dodges, and breakfalls.
- Crawl and Dodge can share the sneak and sprint keys (they need a double-tap when bound together).
- A new advancement for forging an Electrum Lance.
- All leaves slowly frost over and turn white while it snows, then go back to green once the snow stops.
- New enchantments: Vitality, Reach, Volley, Faster Attacks, Bracewalk, Guard Break, Soulbound (kept on death), Seeking,
  Trueshot, Quick Draw, Cavalier, Straight Shot, and Displacement.
- Infernite pickaxes, shovels, axes, hoes, and hammers smelt what they mine. Ores, logs, and crops are left alone, and
  Silk Touch turns it off.
- Added Better Climbing.
- You walk up full blocks without jumping (sneak to stop).
    - Step Up replaces Auto-Jump in the Controls settings.
- A Creative Items tab in Creative mode only with command blocks, spawn eggs, and the other creative-only items.
- Added Cubes Without Borders for borderless fullscreen.
- Added Entity Pin Cushions (replaces Lodged).
- Tree bark can be mixed into pulp.
- Rich Gravel can be crafted from gravel and flint. Placed Rich Gravel always drops itself.
- Dough and butter can be mixed in a Basin or Mechanical Mixer.
- Straw golems harvest sugar cane, cocoa, crops on trellises, and crops on ropes.
- Deserts have occasional dead trees with branches. Branches break into sticks, or drop themselves with Silk Touch.
- Map Atlases and Magnets work from anywhere in your inventory, including worn backpacks.
- Dyed slime balls now show up in EMI, grouped together as Slime Balls.
- Jack-o'-Straws can face any of 16 directions, like heads.
- Many more enchanting tablets (some used from Raspberry Flavoured, with permission).
