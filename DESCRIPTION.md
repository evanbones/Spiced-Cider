## Overview

Spiced Cider is a carefully curated modpack focused on cohesion, progression, and ambience. It's built
on the following ideas:

- **Cohesion:** Mods don't exist in a vacuum. Hundreds of them have been reworked to feel like one game: duplicate
  items are merged, textures share one style, and new content ties into what's already there.

- **Progression:** The early game is longer without being harder. Your first tools come from flint and straw, and
  tools run from Flint to Copper, Bronze and Steel before you ever touch Diamond. Each tier asks a little more of you,
  from hammering ingots into sheets on a Workstone to forging your own armor.

- **Ambience:** Reworked terrain that stretches to the horizon, new cave biomes, shifting cloud cover and wind, and a
  hazier, hotter Nether.

- **Conveyance:** Things should be discoverable. Items come with notes in EMI, every plant, creature and fish you find
  is recorded in your Field Guide, and custom advancement tabs point the way through the pack.

- **Choice:** Many options have tradeoffs. A Backpack gives you 27 more slots but takes your chestplate slot. Gliders
  and ziplines each get you around in their own way, and Enchantment Tablets give niche effects instead of a few
  must-have enchantments.

- **Removed & Replaced:** Villagers, villages and trading are gone. Experience and enchanting tables made way for
  Enchantment Tablets, brewing stands for cauldron brewing, and the smithing table for the Anvil and Styling Table.
